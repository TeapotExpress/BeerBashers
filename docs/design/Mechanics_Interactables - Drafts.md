\[Subjects to change \- this is a draft, one of possible many\]

Possible pick ups:

1. Drinks:  
   **Hot drink** \- Makes you run faster for a short while  
   **Cold drink** \- Throw it at other players to slow them for a short while  
   **Refreshing drink** \- Makes the *Stamina* bar recharge faster

2. Furniture:  
   **Chairs** / **Stools** \- can be picked up and thrown at players to knock them over, dealing 1 *\[container\]* to them  
     
3. **Food** \- A plate of food that when eaten restore *1 \[container\]* to the player  
     
4. **“The Premium Spirit”** \- the last barrel of “spirit” in the inn, *the goal of the game is to hold it for the longest time / Holding it gives the player points slowly until one player reaches the established point limit*   
     
   

Possible mechanics:

1. **Stamina** \- A bar that allows players to run faster until depleted. It recharges when not used.  
2. **Health** / **“Stars”** \- Each player has 3 *\[containers\]* which represent their “health”/ ”stun” bar. When depleted, a player will be stunned for a *\[short period of time\]*. Players are not able to be hit for a brief moment after suffering one  
3. **Holding ”The Premium Spirit”** \- Players holding the barrel are slowed, and use more stamina when running.  
4. **Picking up items** \- A player can hold 1 item, by walking over to an item and pressing the *\[interact key\]* to pick it up. If an item is a **DRINK** or **FOOD**, pressing the *\[interact key\]* again will use the item.  
5. **Throwing items** \- Items such as *“The Premium Spirit”* and Furniture can be thrown at other players to throw them off balance. While holding a Furniture item or *“The Premium Spirit”* and pressing the *\[throw key\]* will make the player throw it in the direction they are facing. If the thrown item collides with a player, the struck character will stop for a moment and drop whatever item they would be carrying onto the floor.
